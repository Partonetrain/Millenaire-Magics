This is a big update from 0.8.1.3, world backup recommended if you are updating from that version, but not a major update from 0.8.2 alpha.

- Updated Millenaire to 9.1.1
- Updated Cle to 9.1.0-2.0.5
- Updated OBE to 1.2.6, supermartijn642corelib 1.1.26a, to Titanium to 4.0.56, Architectury to 13.0.12, Iris Bonk to 0.1.4, FindMe to 3.3.5, Supplementaries to 4.1.2, Vista to 5.6.9, OctoLib to 0.6.3.2, SeasonHud to 2.0.12, ThirstWasReclaimed to 3.0.7, Entity Culling to 1.12.1, Everycomp to 2.11.58, FTB Chunks to 2101.1.24, Particular Reforged to 1.6.1, JEED to 2.3.6, Serene Seasons Plus to 5.2.4.1, NERB to 0.6.2,
- Removed Anvil Never Too Expensive as Easy Anvils has an equivalent option. In practice the behavior is different but Easy Anvils capping it is preferred.
- Added Ars Hex Unity
- Malum's and Lodestone's magic damage attributes have been reverted back to their original names (Spirit Damage -> Magic Damage) and are now compatible with Ars spells
