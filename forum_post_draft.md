#Millenaire Magics modpack: Playtesters Wanted!

"*Somehow, ancient civilizations have found themselves whisked away into a world of cubes and magic...*"

Millénaire Magics is a highly customized Minecraft 1.21.1 modpack designed to challenge you, encourage you to explore new forms of magic, and gain a reputation for helping multiple cultures build themselves a new life in the Minecraft world.
For full details, please check out the [CurseForge page](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/modpacks/millenaire-magics).

Hi, I'm Train, one of Quark's maintainers, and for the last several months, I've been working on this modpack. It's finally at a point where I'm seeking constructive criticism from the public. **I want to be clear that this is not an official Violet Moon Modpack:tm:, but rather a modpack made by a member of the Violet Moon team. Vazkii has given his blessing for me to discuss and promote it here.**

If you are looking to create a throwaway world to test, I added a command to quick-start a survival scenario (cheats required): `/testing_platform`
It spawns a platform with command blocks that give you unbreakable iron items and other stuff to skip the early game survival struggle.

Specifically, I am looking for feedback on:
- Integrations. I have taken special care (and written a lot of code) to make this modpack feel like a whole experience rather than mods just thrown together. Of course with the nature of Minecraft content mods they're largely seperate experiences, but my job is to create an experience you couldn't find from just throwing mods together.
- Millenaire village generation/biome placement:
	- Millenaire has a brand new placement system that attempts to find flat terrain to place villages on, and it has taken a lot of tweaking from my addon Cle to make the placement feel reasonable. 9.1.1 update helped but further tweaks may be required.
	- Are they too frequent? Too rare? 
	- Are there particular biomes in the [spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1XXQG44UhEQqV-bs55lnX_1UHeXsB6FknHgDkZggx8FA/edit?gid=0#gid=0) that always fail to get villages placed in? 
	- Is the biome distribution (again, in the [spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1XXQG44UhEQqV-bs55lnX_1UHeXsB6FknHgDkZggx8FA/edit?gid=0#gid=0)) intuitive? I spread them out and tried to make cultues match their historically accurate regions to the best of my knowledge but I am not exactly a history buff and of course sacrifices had to be made for gameplay reasons.
- Performance. Millages are currently set to chunkload themselves when no player is nearby, but cannot be claimed by FTB Chunks.
- Millenaire gathering/crafting/production chains. I get the impression there may be some broken links in the mod as it comes, but this can be fixed in config. It's intentional that only Byzantines and Seljuks can mine iron by default, but I'm not convinced this is good.
- Difficulty. I made it to my own tastes/accounting for the general high power level of mid-to-late-game Ars and Malum but it may need tweaking.
- Loot balance. The modpack has tons of customized loot tables to make dungeoneering rewarding, but I'm worried it may be too much.
- Tips/documentation. Although this is a heavily modded pack (not a kitchen sink though) I want it to be approachable to modded newcomers. That said I am not interested in having FTB Quests as a guide, the pack works much better as an evergreen experience.
- "Personality". I tend to put some minor extraneous/funny/meme stuff in my work, to make things interesting to come across. I try to make these things non-invasive but it might come off as "too much"/confusing.

Feedback can be directed here or the [issue tracker](https://github.com/Partonetrain/Millenaire-Magics/issues)

Thank you for playing!
