# Tutorial

Thank you for playing **ParCool**!
ParCool is a mod to enable you to *Cool* actions like *Parkour*.
Now you become a parkour-player. Let's run around the Minecraft World!

In this page, I'll introduce contents which you should play first!

## Stamina

First I have to introduce Stamina system.

You have a new bar called Stamina. This represents your short-term fatigue.

Stamina is displayed above your thirst bar.

Most ParCool actions consume stamina, and it recovers in relatively short time while resting.
You experience some penalties when stamina is depleted, and can't use any ParCool actions until it regenerates.

## Fast Run

[Fast Run](parcool:actions/fast_run.md) is the most basic movement of ParCool. You can run faster than a normal sprint.

Fast Run is the base of many other actions.

Hold <key name="key.sprint"/> while moving to maintain a Fast Run.

## Vault

There are some actions derived from Fast Run. One of them is [Vault](parcool:actions/vault.md).

It's automatically triggered by colliding to blocks while fast running.

Also, Vault makes it possible to get over fences.

### Technique - In-air Vault

You can vault in air. By utilizing in-air vault it is possible to get over 2-block-high obstacles quickly. It may look
difficult, but actually it's quite easy.

## Hang On

There are times when you might want to climb up higher walls or obstacles. So it's time to
use the [Hang On](parcool:actions/hang_on.md) action!

It's easy to use. Just press <key name="key.parcool.hang"/> key and touch edge of blocks.

Additionally, You can [Climb Up](parcool:actions/climb_up.md) to jump up onto the block by
pressing <key name="key.jump"/>. Thanks to this, it's even possible to overcome 3 block height obstacles!

ParCool allows getting over 5-block-high obstacles with creative use of different actions. Read each page for details on specific actions.

## Breakfall

Here's one more important feature. [Breakfall](parcool:actions/breakfall).

You can take less fall damage by using the breakfall action!

Just press <key name="key.parcool.breakfall"/> key on landing.

## Zipline

Let me introduce you one more thing. It's the [Zipline](parcool:zipline.md).

To use it we have to craft some items.

First let's craft Zipline Hook! There are 2 variants, and the only difference is cosmetic.

<recipe id="parcool:wooden_zipline_hook"/>
<recipe id="parcool:iron_zipline_hook"/>

After crafting, place the hooks positions where you want to.

Next let's craft Zipline Rope.

<recipe id="parcool:zipline_rope"/>

It is possible to change its color by dyeing. Craft it with dye like leather armor.

Press shift + right click to select rope tension. After that, use it on 2 zipline hooks.

Then, start the [Ride Zipline](parcool:actions/ride_zipline.md) action by pressing <key name="key.parcool.hang"/>!
