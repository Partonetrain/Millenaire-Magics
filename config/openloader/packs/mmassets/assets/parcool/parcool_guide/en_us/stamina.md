# Stamina

You have a new bar called Stamina. This represents your short-term fatigue.

Stamina is displayed above your thirst bar.

Most ParCool actions consume stamina, and it recovers in relatively short time while resting.
You experience some penalties when stamina is depleted, and can't use any ParCool actions until it regenerates.

You can stop consuming stamina by drinking potions. See [Potions](parcool:potions.md) for details.
